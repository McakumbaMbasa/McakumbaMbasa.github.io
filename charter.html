Agile Team Charter

Our intern training team charter

A structured summary of the shared intern training approach at Kinetic Skunk IT Solutions, supported by my own daily Scrum and time-tracking evidence.

01 · Team charter

Purpose & Core Values

Purpose: Build employable technical skills through guided, hands-on development, software testing and automation activities.

Core values: Continuous learning and improvement, integrity, mutual trust, accountability, clear communication, and quality-focused software delivery.

02 · Team charter

Communication Protocols

Microsoft TeamsPrimary team communication and daily Scrum updates; supported by my supplied Scrum screenshot.
KimaiDaily work time and activity tracking; supported by my October calendar screenshot.
OutlookScheduling and formal messages within the shared internship communication approach.
TelegramQuick synchronization channel listed in the shared team charter.

Daily Scrum: 09:00–09:30 each working morning, based on my own meeting schedule. The shared example provided by my colleague lists a slightly different time; I have retained my stated schedule.

03 · Team charter

Team Roles

Interns: Learn new technologies, complete assigned training tasks, track time, raise blockers, demonstrate practical skills, and communicate progress.

Facilitator: Assign training material, explain requirements, provide technical guidance and feedback, and evaluate demonstrations.

04 · Team charter

Definition of Done (DoD)

Following the shared internship charter, a training activity is considered ready to be marked done when:

  1. The code or automated test works: the required script, project or test can execute successfully and demonstrate the intended outcome.
  2. The intern demonstrates understanding: the intern can explain and demonstrate the work in a one-on-one review with the facilitator, where required.

Supporting records: GitLab commits, demonstrations, screenshots and Kimai time entries can help document the activity, but are not additional official sign-off criteria in the shared DoD text.

05 · Team charter

Working Agreements

  • Share progress, the next task, and blockers during the daily Scrum.
  • Follow the sprint learning requirements and complete assigned practical work.
  • Ask for facilitator guidance when technical issues cannot be resolved independently.
  • Use GitLab for source-control and submission evidence where relevant.
  • Record actual work activities and working time in Kimai.
  • Communicate respectfully and follow the organization’s workplace practices.

Evidence and authorship: The purpose, communication outline and two DoD points follow the shared internship charter supplied as a reference. This is my own portfolio presentation; the Scrum, GitLab, Kimai and attendance records included here are my evidence. It is not presented as a separately signed company policy.
